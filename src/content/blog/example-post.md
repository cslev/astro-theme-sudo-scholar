This is a placeholder post. Delete it once you've written your own —
it exists to demonstrate the blog collection's frontmatter fields and the
shared post layout (hero image or auto-generated fallback, table of
contents, author byline, tags).

See `docs/blog-post-authoring.md` for the full breakdown of what each
frontmatter field does, and two easy-to-miss Markdown gotchas (apostrophes
in YAML frontmatter, and literal `$` signs in prose) that can silently
corrupt a post if you're not aware of them.
