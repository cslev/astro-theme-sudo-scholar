A second placeholder post, so the blog index, the tag sidebar, and the
archive page all have more than one entry to actually demonstrate their
layout with. Delete both example posts once you've written your own.

Unlike `example-post.md`, this one sets `updatedDate` (so the byline shows
a "last updated" note) and omits `toc: true` — a post with few or no
headings usually doesn't need one, and leaving the field out is enough to
skip it entirely.

## Still gets a heading

Even without a table of contents, headings still work for structuring the
post and anchor-linking — the table of contents is just an optional
navigation aid rendered from them, not a requirement for using them.
